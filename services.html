Common Experiences

When we feel stuck and unfilled in our personal lives and intimate relationships, it is normal to experience:

  • irritation and frustration
  • anxiety, stress, fear
  • difficulty stopping or controlling worry, difficulty falling or staying asleep
  • fatigue, burnout
  • helplessness, hopelessness
  • depression
  • low self-esteem and critical thoughts
