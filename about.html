About

Chelsea Carter, LMFT

I love feeling warm.

You can find me lying under the sun, sitting by the fire, sipping tea, and soaking in warm bodies of water.

Warmth feels special to me because it soothes and relaxes my body.

When I feel warm, I feel present, happy, satisfied, and ready to connect with myself and other people.

I also love to snorkel (which, unfortunately, can be a very cold activity)!
